Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Lamplight Jazz Agent ("the App") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Lamplight Jazz YouTube channel ("we"). It is used only to publish videos to that single channel. This policy explains what data the App accesses through YouTube API Services and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the App accesses

  • Upload permission (youtube.upload): to upload videos and thumbnails that the channel owner has reviewed and approved.
  • Read-only channel information (youtube.readonly): the channel name and identifier, and the processing and privacy status of videos the App uploaded, to confirm that a scheduled video went public.

The App does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers' personal information, messages or any other user's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the channel owner's own videos and to confirm their publication status. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or combined with other data.

Storage and security

The OAuth authorisation token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer or server, outside any public code repository, and is used solely by the App. Video identifiers and publication dates are kept in the App's private database to maintain a production history.

Retention and deletion

Authorisation data is kept only while the channel owner uses the App. It is deleted when access is revoked or upon request. Data obtained from YouTube API Services is not stored for longer than needed and is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, except the identifiers of videos the App uploaded.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
